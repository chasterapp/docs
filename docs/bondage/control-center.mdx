The control center holds the settings of the [session screen](/bondage/session-screen): text size, sound, full screen, the status of your device, and the session rules. Open it from the status tray in the top corner of the session screen. When something needs your attention (sound blocked, screen able to turn off, low battery), the tray shows how many alerts there are.

<img src="/img/bondage-control-center.png" alt={`Control center`} className="img-phone" />

The control center has three parts:

- **Needs attention**: anything that could stop the screen from keeping you safe, with a quick fix such as **Try again**, **Turn on** or **Check sound**.
- **This device**: text size, sound, full screen, status and pinning. These are "Saved on this screen only": each phone, tablet or computer keeps its own.
- **Session rules**: the lock's safety settings, "same on every screen".

<img src="/img/bondage-control-center-needs-attention.png" alt={`Needs attention rows in the control center`} className="img-phone" />

You can move through it with arrow keys or a remote: **OK** selects, **Back** goes back.

## Before you start

Run through this list before you lock your restraints:

1. Put the device where you can **see and reach it** from your position.
2. **Plug it in.** Long sessions drain the battery, and the screen stays on.
3. Turn **sound on** and run the [delayed sound test](#test-the-sound) from where you will be.
4. Check that **Screen on** is **On**, or set your phone to never auto-lock.
5. **Pin the screen** so you can't leave it by accident: [Guided Access on iPhone](#iphone-and-ipad-guided-access), [App pinning on Android](#android-app-pinning).
6. Check that **Status** reads **All working**.

## Text size

**Text size** makes the whole session screen bigger or smaller, so you can read it from where you are. There are 11 sizes. The default depends on your device (phone, tablet, computer or TV). Select the row to go back to the default.

![Text size setting](/img/bondage-control-center-text-size.png)

## Status

**Status** reads **All working**, or the number of problems. It checks:

| Row | What it means | If it's not OK |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Updates** | **Live**: changes from your keyholder show up as they happen. | **Not updating**: check your connection. Your release needs the internet. |
| **Screen on** | **On**: the screen won't go to sleep while the session is open. | **Off**: tap to keep it on. **Unsupported**: your browser can't keep the screen on; set your device's auto-lock to Never. |
| **Battery** | The battery level, and whether it's charging. | Low at 20% or less: plug in your charger. Some browsers, including Safari, don't share the battery level and show **Unavailable**. |
| **Leave protection** | **Asks before leaving**: the browser asks before this page closes. | |

![Status rows](/img/bondage-control-center-status.png)

With sound on, the voice also warns you when the connection is lost for more than 30 seconds, when the battery reaches 20% and 10%, and when the screen may turn off.

## Full screen

**Full screen** hides the browser bars and tabs. It appears only where the browser supports it, which excludes Safari on iPhone. On iPhone, [installing Chaster on your home screen](/install-on-your-phone) gives a similar result.

## Keep the screen on

The session screen asks your device to keep the screen awake while it is open. **Screen on** in [Status](#status) shows whether it worked. The device may still turn the screen off if:

- your browser doesn't support it (**Unsupported**);
- you switch tabs or apps (the screen asks again when you come back);
- the battery saver steps in.

As a backup, set your device not to turn off the screen during the session:

- **iPhone and iPad**: **Settings › Display & Brightness › Auto-Lock › Never**. With Guided Access, you can set this for the Guided Access session only, see below.
- **Android**: **Settings › Display › Screen timeout**, set it to the longest value. Some phones keep the screen on while charging with **Developer options › Stay awake**.
- **Computer**: plug it in and turn off sleep in the power settings.

Remember to change these settings back after your session.

## Keep this screen in front

The control center's **Keep this screen in front** row shows how to pin the screen on your device. Pinning locks the device to the session screen, so a stray tap or a notification can't take you out of it and silently stop your check-ins.

### iPhone and iPad: Guided Access

<img src="/img/bondage-control-center-pinning-ios.png" alt={`Pinning guidance on iPhone`} className="img-phone" />

Set it up once:

1. Open **Settings › Accessibility › Guided Access** and turn **Guided Access** on.
2. In **Passcode Settings**, set a passcode, and turn on **Face ID** or **Touch ID** so you can end it easily.
3. Optionally, in **Display Auto-Lock**, choose **Never**: the screen stays on during Guided Access only.

For each session:

1. Open the session screen.
2. Triple-click the **side button** (or the Home button on devices that have one).
3. Tap **Start**.

To end Guided Access, triple-click the side button again and enter your passcode or use Face ID or Touch ID, then tap **End**.

### Android: App pinning

<img src="/img/bondage-control-center-pinning-android.png" alt={`Pinning guidance on Android`} className="img-phone" />

Set it up once:

1. Open **Settings**, search for **App pinning** (also called **Screen pinning**, often under **Security**), and turn it on.
2. Choose whether to **ask for your PIN before unpinning**.

For each session:

1. Open the session screen.
2. Open **Recent apps** (swipe up and hold, or tap the square button).
3. Tap the icon at the top of your browser's or Chaster's window, then tap **Pin**.

To unpin, swipe up and hold (gesture navigation), or touch and hold **Back** and **Recent apps** together (button navigation).

## Sound

The session screen can play alarms and read your session aloud, so you don't have to look at it. Open **Sound** in the control center.

<img src="/img/bondage-sound-settings-1.png" alt={`Sound settings: master switch, safety and time`} className="img-phone" />

The **Sound** switch turns everything on or off. Turning it off also silences check-in alarms.

On iPhone and iPad, the browser only allows sound after a tap on the page. If sound gets blocked (for example after the phone locked), the screen says "Tap anywhere to turn sound back on".

"Sounds only play while this screen is open and awake. If your phone locks or you switch apps, they stop until you come back." When you come back, the screen sums up what you missed.

### Safety

Always on while sound is on:

- **Check-in alarm**: soft chime, then louder as your grace runs out.
- **Release countdown**: countdown, release, "You're free".
- **Device problems**: connection lost, low battery, screen turning off.

### Time

- **Milestones**: 1 hour, 30, 10, 5 and 1 minute left. On by default.
- **Final countdown**: the last 10 seconds. On by default.
- **Time updates**: says how much time is left at a regular interval: **Milestones only** (default), or every 5, 10, 15, 30 or 60 minutes.

When your timer is hidden, the voice never says the time left: it says how long you've been in session instead.

<img src="/img/bondage-sound-settings-2.png" alt={`Sound settings: lock events, shared link, voice and test`} className="img-phone" />

### Lock events

Each can be turned off separately. All are on by default except **Spoken detail**.

- **Keyholder actions**: time added or removed, freeze, timer hidden.
- **Extensions and games**: wheel of fortune, dice, random events, penalties.
- **Tasks and verification**: new task, task done, picture requested.
- **Pillory**: put in and out of the pillory.
- **Spoken detail**: also dice values, wheel spins, check-in confirmations and setup changes.

### Shared link

- **Votes**: a summary of the votes on your [shared link](/features/shared-links).
- **Summary every**: how often votes are read out: 1, 5 (default) or 15 minutes.

### Voice

- **Voice**: the voice comes from your device's system voices.
- **Say my keyholder as**: how the voice calls your keyholder, for example a nickname. Leave it empty for "Your keyholder".
- **Read my code aloud when I'm free**: off by default. Anyone nearby will hear it.

### Test the sound

- **Test sound** plays the check-in alarm and voice now.
- **Test in 20 seconds** gives you time to walk back to where you will be, to check you can hear it from there.

After the test, answer **Did you hear it clearly?**. If you answer **No**, the screen suggests fixes for your device, under **Try this, then test again**.

<img src="/img/bondage-sound-test.png" alt={`Sound test: Did you hear it clearly?`} className="img-phone" />

<img src="/img/bondage-sound-test-fixes.png" alt={`Fixes suggested after answering No`} className="img-phone" />

### Sound troubleshooting

- **iPhone silent**: flip the Ring/Silent switch, or turn off Silent mode in Control Center. Raise the volume while the page is open. Check that sound isn't going to AirPods or a Bluetooth speaker.
- **Android silent**: raise **Media volume**, not only the ringer, and turn off Do Not Disturb.
- **No voice for your language on this device**: add a voice in your device's accessibility settings (on iPhone, **Settings › Accessibility › Read & Speak › Voices**; on Android, search Settings for **text-to-speech**), then reload the page. You can also switch Chaster to a language your device has a voice for, in **Settings › General › Language**.
- **Your browser hides the device voices**: a privacy setting or content blocker is hiding them. In **Brave**, tap the lion icon and turn Shields off for Chaster, or open Chaster in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android).

## Session rules

**Session rules** shows the lock's [bondage safety settings](/bondage/create-a-bondage-lock#bondage-safety-settings), and who set them:

- **Emergency release**: the countdown, or Off.
- **Check-ins**: "Every 5 min, 2 min to answer", or Off.
- **Auto-unlock**: On or Off.

![Session rules](/img/bondage-session-rules.png)

If you have the **Edit the bondage safety settings** permission, select a rule to change the settings.

<img src="/img/bondage-session-rules-modal.png" alt={`Changing the session rules`} className="img-phone" />

Otherwise, it reads "Only *your keyholder* can change this." or "This can't be changed during the session." Changes apply at once, on every screen.
