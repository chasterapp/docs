The session screen is where a bondage lock runs. It shows your timer in large type you can read from across the room, runs your safety check-ins, and releases you when the time is up. Open it right after locking your restraints, and keep it open until you are free.

## Open the session screen

On **Locks**, the card of a running bondage lock has an **Open bondage session** button. It opens the session screen.

![Lock card of a bondage lock with the Open bondage session button](/img/bondage-lock-card.png)

Only the wearer can open it. A keyholder, or a lock that isn't a running bondage lock, is sent back to the locks page.

## Keep the screen open

:::danger Check-ins and automatic release only run on this screen
Safety check-ins, the emergency release and automatic unlock run on your device, on this page. Your lock stays locked if you leave it, but:

- if you **close the page or go back**, check-ins stop. The next time you open the screen, the check-in cycle starts again from zero;
- if your **phone locks, sleeps, or you switch apps**, the browser pauses the page. When you come back, the screen catches up, and if a check-in was missed in the meantime, you are released at that moment;
- if you **lose your connection**, a release can't reach Chaster until it's back.

Keep the device on, unlocked, charged and connected for the whole session, and pin the screen. See [Control center](/bondage/control-center#keep-the-screen-on).
:::

When you try to leave, the screen asks first:

> **Leave the session screen?** Your lock stays on. Check-ins and the emergency release only work on this screen.

![Leave the session screen? confirmation](/img/bondage-session-leave-dialog.png)

## The main screen

![Bondage session screen on a phone](/img/bondage-session-main.png)

From top to bottom:

- **Header**: "Self-lock", or your keyholder's name and avatar. A dot shows **Live** when the screen is up to date, or **Not updating** when it can't reach Chaster. The status tray on the right opens the [control center](/bondage/control-center) and shows how many things need your attention.
- **Timer**: the **Time remaining** until the end of your lock. The timer reads **Frozen** with a snowflake when your timer is frozen.
- **Locked**: how long you have been locked.
- **Ends**: when your lock ends: a time if it's today, a weekday and time within the week, a date otherwise. It is hidden when the timer is frozen or hidden, and on small phones.
- **Events**: what just happened on your lock: time added or removed, freeze, extensions, tasks, verification pictures, votes. Larger screens show a board of recent events; phones show the latest one.
- **Emergency release** and **Check-in** buttons, at the bottom where they are easy to reach. The check-in button shows the time left before your next check-in.

On a tablet or computer, the same screen uses the extra space for a board you can read from a distance.

![Bondage session screen on a tablet](/img/bondage-session-main-tablet.png)

The screen updates every few seconds and as soon as your keyholder or an extension changes your lock.

### Hidden timer

If your timer is hidden, the timer shows **Timer hidden** with an eye icon instead of the remaining time, and the end time is hidden. The voice never says how much time is left: it says how long you've been in session instead.

![Session screen with a hidden timer](/img/bondage-session-hidden-timer.png)

## Safety check-ins

When the dead man's switch is on, the screen asks you to confirm you're OK at the check-in interval set on the lock (every 5 minutes by default).

1. The **Check-in** button counts down to the next check-in. You can tap it at any time to check in early and restart the interval.
2. When the interval is up, the screen switches to **Safety check-in required**, with a **Confirm within** countdown: "Confirm you're OK to keep the session going."
3. Tap **I'm OK**. The countdown resets, and the voice confirms when the next check-in is due.
4. If the grace period runs out without an answer, the lock is released ("Check-in missed. Releasing the lock.") and the screen shows your combination.

![Safety check-in required screen](/img/bondage-session-checkin-alarm.png)

With sound on, the alarm gets more insistent as the grace period runs out: a soft chime every 10 seconds, then every 5 seconds once half the grace period is gone, then every second for the last 20 seconds, with spoken reminders ("1 minute left to check in."). The first alarm of a session says where to tap: "Check-in. Tap the bottom of the screen if you're OK."

A missed check-in counts as an emergency unlock: the lock history shows it as one, and your keyholder is notified that you used the emergency unlock.

When the dead man's switch is off, there is no **Check-in** button and no alarm.

## Emergency release

The **Emergency release** button appears when emergency unlock is on for the lock. It is for ending the session early, at any time, for any reason.

1. Tap **Emergency release**. There is no extra confirmation: the countdown starts at once.
2. The screen shows **Releasing in** with the countdown (15 seconds by default, set on the lock), and your **Safe phrase**.
3. To change your mind, tap **Cancel, stay locked** before the end of the countdown.
4. At the end of the countdown, the lock is released and your combination appears.

![Emergency release countdown](/img/bondage-session-emergency.png)

With sound on, the countdown ticks, louder in the last 5 seconds, and the voice says "Emergency release in 15 seconds. Tap the bottom of the screen to cancel."

This differs from [emergency unlock](/locks/emergency-unlock) on a chastity lock: you don't type the confirmation phrase (it is only shown, as your safe phrase), you can't leave a message, and the countdown gives you a chance to cancel. Like a chastity emergency unlock, it is recorded in the lock history and your keyholder is notified.

If your keyholder turns emergency unlock off during the countdown, the countdown stops and you go back to the session. A check-in that falls due during the countdown waits until you cancel it.

## End of the timer

When your timer ends (or the maximum lock time is reached), what happens depends on **Automatic unlock**:

- **On** (default): the screen unlocks you by itself, within seconds, and shows your combination. If it can't reach Chaster, it retries.
- **Off**: the screen shows **Ready to unlock**, "Your timer is up. Unlock to reveal your combination." Tap **Unlock**.

![Ready to unlock screen](/img/bondage-session-ready.png)

If a release fails to reach the server, the screen shows "The release couldn't reach the server. Check your connection and retry." with a **Retry release** button.

## You're free

Once you are released, by the timer, your keyholder, an emergency release or a missed check-in, the screen shows **You're free** and your combination:

- a code is shown in large characters with **Copy code**. Codes are case-sensitive.
- a combination photo can be tapped to enlarge.

![You're free screen with the combination](/img/bondage-session-released.png)

With sound on, the voice says "You're free. Your combination is on screen." and repeats it after 1 and 5 minutes until you tap the screen. You can ask the voice to read your code aloud too, see [Control center](/bondage/control-center#voice).

Tap **Close** to leave. The combination stays available on your lock page afterwards, as for any lock.
