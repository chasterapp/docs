Besides creating your own bondage lock, you can join one created by a keyholder. Bondage shared locks are marked with a violet **Bondage** chip everywhere they appear.

:::info Beta
The bondage pages of Explore and the lock type search filter are only available to accounts in the bondage beta.
:::

## Explore

On **Explore**, a **Lock type** switch at the top toggles between **All** and **Bondage**.

![Explore with the All / Bondage switch](/img/bondage-explore-lens.png)

**Bondage** opens a page dedicated to bondage sessions:

- **Restraints on. Timer holds the key.** An introduction to bondage on Chaster, with **Start a bondage lock** to [create your own](/bondage/create-a-bondage-lock).
- **Safety, set per session**: auto-release, emergency release and check-ins, and a reminder that each lock shows which ones it uses.
- **The founding sessions**: the first public bondage locks.

![Bondage page of Explore](/img/bondage-explore-page.png)

On **All**, a **Bondage locks** card introduces the feature. You can close it with **Dismiss**: it won't come back on your account.

![Bondage locks introduction card](/img/bondage-education-card.png)

## Search

The search filters have a **Lock type** filter: **All** (every type), **Chastity** (timer & keyholder) or **Bondage** (restraints & release). Searching from the Bondage page of Explore sets it to **Bondage**. The same filter exists in your favorite shared locks.

![Lock type search filter](/img/bondage-search-filter.png)

## The shared lock page

A bondage shared lock page has a **Bondage safety** section listing the safety settings you agree to by joining:

- **Emergency release**: **On** with its countdown, **Off** ("You can't end this session early yourself."), or **Your choice** when the keyholder lets you decide when you join.
- **Safety check-ins**: for example "Check-in every 5 min. If you don't confirm within 2 min, an emergency release is triggered.", or **Off** ("You won't be prompted to confirm you're OK.").
- **Automatic unlock**: **On**, or **Off** ("When your timer ends, you'll unlock manually from the session screen.").

![Bondage safety section of a shared lock page](/img/bondage-public-lock-safety.png)

Below, it says who can change these settings during the session: you ("You'll be able to adjust these settings at any time from the session screen.") or only the keyholder.

:::warning Read the warning
**Fewer safety options on this session** means the lock has no emergency release or no check-ins. Only join if you have another safe way out of your restraints.
:::

Every bondage lock has a maximum lock time: whatever happens to the timer, the session ends by then.

## Join a bondage lock

Joining works as for any shared lock. The review step shows the bondage safety summary again: "Here's how your session with *keyholder* will run. Take a moment to review before you start." If the keyholder lets you choose emergency release, decide there.

After joining, lock your restraints, then tap **Open bondage session** on the lock card. See [Session screen](/bondage/session-screen).

## Shared links

When the wearer of a bondage lock shares their [shared link](/features/shared-links), the voting page speaks of a bondage session instead of a chastity device: "*username*'s bondage timer", "Add time to keep them tied up longer, or remove time to free them sooner." Link previews use the same title.

![Shared link vote page of a bondage lock](/img/bondage-shared-link-vote.png)

## Your calendar

Bondage days appear in violet in the activity calendar of your profile and statistics, with a **Bondage** legend. A day with both a chastity and a bondage lock is split in two colors.
