A bondage lock is a timed restraint session. You lock your restraints (cuffs, rope, a lockbox holding their keys), and Chaster keeps the combination sealed until the timer ends. While the session runs, a full-screen **session screen** shows your timer, asks you to confirm you're OK, and releases you when the time is up.

:::info Beta
Bondage locks are in beta and only available to selected accounts. If you don't see **Lock type** when you create a lock, bondage locks aren't enabled on your account yet.
:::

:::danger Your safety comes first
Chaster cannot physically free you. A release only reveals a combination: you still need to be able to reach your lock or lockbox and open it, and Chaster needs a working internet connection to do so. Never do anything in restraints that you could not get out of if your phone died, your connection dropped or the app failed. Always keep an independent way out (safety shears, a spare key) within reach.

Safety check-ins and automatic release only work **while the session screen is open and awake** on your device. If you close it, switch apps or let the phone sleep, nobody is watching. See [Session screen](/bondage/session-screen#keep-the-screen-open).
:::

## How a bondage session works

1. **Create the lock.** Choose **Bondage** as the lock type, set your duration, and review the bondage safety settings. A maximum lock time is required. See [Create a bondage lock](/bondage/create-a-bondage-lock).
2. **Lock your restraints.** Lock them with the combination Chaster gives you, exactly as with a regular lock.
3. **Open the session screen.** On the lock card, tap **Open bondage session**. Put your phone or tablet where you can see and reach it, keep it charged, and pin the screen. See [Control center](/bondage/control-center).
4. **Play.** The timer runs. If safety check-ins are on, the screen regularly asks you to tap **I'm OK**. Your keyholder, extensions and shared-link visitors can change your time as usual.
5. **Get released.** When the timer ends, the screen unlocks you and shows your combination. You can also end the session early with **Emergency release**, if it is available on your lock.

## Bondage vs chastity locks

Bondage locks use the same lock engine as chastity locks: timer, keyholder, permissions, extensions, shared links, test locks and history all work the same way. These are the differences:

| | Chastity lock | Bondage lock |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What you lock | A chastity device | Restraints, or a lockbox holding their keys |
| During the lock | The regular lock page | A full-screen [session screen](/bondage/session-screen) designed to be read from a distance |
| Maximum lock time | Optional | **Required**, and it can never be removed. See [Maximum lock time](/bondage/create-a-bondage-lock#maximum-lock-time) |
| Safety check-ins | None | Optional "dead man's switch": miss a check-in and you are released |
| Emergency unlock | Type a confirmation phrase to unlock | A short countdown you can cancel, started from the session screen. See [Emergency release](/bondage/session-screen#emergency-release) |
| End of the timer | You unlock from the lock page | The session screen can unlock you automatically |
| Sounds and voice | None | Alarms, time updates and lock events read aloud |
| Safety settings during the lock | None | Editable from the session screen with the **Bondage safety** permission |
| [Verification picture](/extensions/verification-picture#bondage-locks) | Shows the chastity device, with optional numbered seals | Shows the restraints from a checklist, no seals, code in the photo off by default |
| Calendar | Chastity days | Bondage days, in violet |

Everything else, including every other extension, behaves exactly as on a chastity lock.

## Bondage safety settings

Every bondage lock carries three safety settings. They are set when the lock is created and shown to anyone joining a shared lock:

- **Safety check-ins** (dead man's switch): every few minutes, the session screen asks you to confirm you're OK. If you don't answer in time, the lock releases itself. By default, a check-in comes every 5 minutes, with 2 more minutes to answer.
- **Emergency release**: when emergency unlock is on, the session screen has an **Emergency release** button that unlocks you after a short countdown (15 seconds by default) that you can cancel.
- **Automatic unlock**: when the timer ends, the session screen unlocks you and shows your combination without a tap.

Details are in [Create a bondage lock](/bondage/create-a-bondage-lock#bondage-safety-settings).
