With shared links, share your lock with other people, and ask them to add or remove time. Configure the lock to require a certain number of visits before you can unlock.

## Configuration

Once your session is created, you will be able to send a shared link to everyone, which you can share on social networks, Discord, or anywhere. On this link, visitors will be able to add or remove you from the time, depending on the time set.

You can configure the time added and removed for each vote. You can also propose to the visitor to vote randomly, so they will have a button to make this choice.

:::info
Each visitor can vote once a day, until midnight in their time zone. The limit applies per IP address and, for signed-in visitors, per account.
:::

## Number of visits required

You can force yourself to share the link and your session with as many people as possible by activating the required number of visits. You will then need to reach the number of votes configured before normal unlocking. An enabled [emergency unlock](/locks/emergency-unlock) can still end the session without those votes.

Finally, you can limit the votes only to logged-in users.

## Show on my profile

:::caution Feature preview
Showing a shared link on your profile is being rolled out progressively. You may not see this option in the app yet.
:::

With **Show on my profile**, anyone visiting your profile can add or remove time from your lock, without opening the shared link.

Tick **Show on my profile** in the Share links settings when you set up your lock. When a keyholder configures the lock, the option reads **Show on the wearer's profile**.

![Share links settings with the "Show on my profile" option ticked](/img/shared-links-config-show-on-profile.png)

On a shared lock with the option on, wearers see **Usable from your profile** on the Share links card of the lock page before they join.

![Share links settings on a shared lock page with the "Usable from your profile" chip](/img/shared-links-shared-lock-on-profile-chip.png)

You can turn it on or off at any time while the lock is running, with the **Show on my profile** switch on the lock's Share links page. The switch follows the permission described in [Who can change it](#who-can-change-it).

![Show on my profile switch below the vote amounts on the Share links page of a running lock](/img/shared-links-extension-page-profile-switch.png)

Anyone allowed to edit the lock's extensions, with the **Manage extensions** [permission](/locks/permissions), can also change it in the Share links settings: open **Extension settings**, then **Edit extensions**, then configure Share links. Under the Standard preset, no one holds this permission.

![Share links settings of a running lock with the "Show on my profile" option ticked](/img/shared-links-running-config-show-on-profile.png)

### Voting from the profile

While the lock is running, visitors see "You can change this lock's time" on the lock card of your profile, with buttons to add or remove time, and a random button if you allowed it.

![Lock card on a profile with the "You can change this lock's time" strip and its add, remove and random buttons](/img/shared-links-profile-vote-bar.png)

After a vote, the visitor sees a confirmation, then "You can vote again in..." until their next vote is allowed.

![Confirmation "You added 1 hour!" and the "You can vote again in 3 hours" chip on the profile lock card](/img/shared-links-profile-vote-cooldown.png)

Votes from the profile follow the same rules as votes from the link: the same amounts of time, the same daily limit counted per account and per IP address, and the same signed-in restriction if you enabled it. They count toward the number of visits required.

### Who can change it

The **Show or hide the shared link on the wearer's profile** [permission](/locks/permissions) decides who can turn the option on or off. By default, only the wearer can. Under the Full trust preset, or with a custom contract that grants this permission, the keyholder can too. If a custom contract has no setting for this permission, only the wearer holds it.

![Extensions section of the lock permissions with the "Show or hide the shared link on the wearer's profile" row](/img/shared-links-permission-row.png)
