Regularly take a picture of your chastity device, or of your restraints on a [bondage lock](/bondage), to show that you are still locked.

## Introduction

With the Verification picture extension, prove to your keyholder and others that you are locked in your chastity device, or still restrained on a bondage lock. When you launch a verification, a six digit code is generated. You include this verification code in your photo next to your device or restraints. You can write it on paper, display it on a device screen, or use a timestamp application, whichever method you and your keyholder prefer. This proves that the photo was just taken and verifies the authenticity of your submission.

Whether a code is required depends on the [Code in the photo](#code-in-the-photo) setting.

You can choose to check your session regularly with the non-cumulative mode, or whenever you want. The keyholder can also ask you for verification if he wants.

## Verification

You can enable verification to require that your submitted images are reviewed and validated before being accepted. When configuring verification, you can choose who can validate your submissions:

- **Community members**: Allows other members of the community to review and validate your images through a voting process
- **Only my keyholder**: Restricts validation to your keyholder only, keeping the verification process private

To learn more about how verification works, including the validation process and requirements for each mode, refer to the [Verification](/features/verification) documentation.

## Initial verification

You can enable the "Require an initial verification" option to automatically request a verification picture as soon as the lock starts. This lets you prove that you are locked from the very beginning of your session, without waiting for your keyholder to ask for a verification.

If the extension is added to a lock that is already running, the initial verification is requested as soon as the extension is added.

## Code in the photo

The **Code in the photo** setting decides whether the wearer must show a fresh code in each picture: "The wearer writes or shows a fresh code in each photo, so reviewers know it was just taken."

![Code in the photo setting](/img/verification-picture-code-setting.png)

- On **chastity** locks, it is on by default. Turning it off shows a warning: "Without a code, reviewers can't tell an old photo from a new one."
- On **bondage** locks, it is off by default, because writing or holding up a code is hard while tied up. Turning it on shows: "Hard to do while tied up. Most bondage locks turn this off and rely on the restraint checklist."

![Code in the photo turned on for a bondage lock, with its hint](/img/verification-picture-code-setting-bondage-hint.png)

When the code is off:

- the upload screen shows no code and only asks for a clear picture;
- reviewers don't check a code, only the device or restraints;
- the picture shows a **No code** chip in the verification history;
- the lock's public settings show "Verification code: No code in the photo".

![Verification history item with the No code chip](/img/verification-picture-no-code-chip.png)

:::info
The **Code in the photo** setting is being rolled out progressively. If you don't see it, a code is always generated and required, on every lock type.
:::

## Bondage locks

On a [bondage lock](/bondage), the extension verifies your restraints instead of a chastity device.

### Restraint checklist

The settings have a **Restraint checklist**: "Restraints the wearer must show in their verification picture." Pick up to 10 restraints from the suggestions (handcuffs, wrist cuffs, ankle cuffs, rope on wrists, rope on ankles, ball gag, blindfold, collar, hogtie, spreader bar, chest harness, hood, leash) or type your own. The keyholder sees it as **Restraints your wearer must show**.

![Restraint checklist in the Verification picture settings](/img/verification-picture-restraint-checklist.png)

The checklist is only used on bondage locks. If the lock type changes to chastity, it is kept but ignored.

### Taking the picture

The wearer is asked to "Regularly take a picture of your restraints to show that you are still restrained." The picture must clearly show every restraint on the checklist, or, with no checklist, that the wearer is restrained. History items are titled **Restraints**.

![Verification picture upload on a bondage lock](/img/verification-picture-bondage-upload.png)

### Review

Reviewers, whether community members or the keyholder, check the restraints listed on the lock when the picture was submitted, and the code only if one was required. A picture can be rejected because:

- the restraints are not visible or are obscured;
- one or more of the listed restraints are missing;
- the restraints do not appear to be secured or applied;
- the picture is too blurry or dark to confirm the restraints;
- the picture does not match the requested verification.

Reviewers don't judge whether the restraints could be escaped: self-bondage is escapable by design. In the community review, bondage pictures can be filtered with the **Bondage** filter.

Numbered seals are not used on bondage locks.

## Numbered seals

When community verification is enabled, you can add an extra layer of security to your verification pictures by using numbered seals. These tamper-proof, single-use plastic locks provide physical proof that your chastity device has not been removed or tampered with between verification submissions.

:::info
Numbered seals are only available on chastity locks, when community verification is enabled on the Verification Picture extension.
:::

### What are numbered seals?

Numbered seals are tamper-proof plastic locks with unique identification numbers printed on them. Once a seal is installed on your device, it cannot be removed without breaking it. The unique number can be photographed during each verification to prove the device remains continuously secured throughout your lock session.

### How to use numbered seals

#### First verification with a seal

1. Before taking your verification picture, attach a numbered seal to your chastity device alongside your regular locking mechanism
2. When submitting your verification picture, check the option to use a numbered seal
3. Enter the unique number printed on the seal (typically a 6-digit number)
4. Take your verification picture ensuring the seal number, and the verification code if one is required, are clearly visible
5. Submit the picture for peer verification

Validators reviewing your submission will see the seal number you entered and verify it matches the number visible in your image.

#### Subsequent verifications

After your first verification is approved, the seal number is registered to your lock session. For all future verification pictures during this session:

- You must continue to show the same seal number in your photos
- Validators will check that the seal number matches the one registered to your session
- This proves the device has remained continuously locked with the same seal

:::info
The seal number is tied to your lock session. Each time you verify, validators confirm the seal hasn't been changed, providing proof of continuous wear.
:::

### Hygiene opening and seal reset

If you use the [Hygiene opening](/extensions/hygiene-opening) extension to temporarily unlock your device for cleaning, you will need to break the numbered seal to remove the device.

When this happens:

1. After relocking, you can attach a new numbered seal with a different number
2. On your next verification picture submission, you'll be asked to enter the new seal number
3. This new seal will then be registered and used for all future verifications until the next hygiene opening

### Where to get numbered seals

Numbered seals (also called tamper-proof seals or security seals) are available from various online retailers and specialty stores. Look for:

- **Individually numbered plastic seals**: Each seal should have a unique number printed on it
- **Single-use design**: The seal should be designed to break when removed
- **Compatible size**: Ensure the seal can fit through the holes in your chastity device

Common sources include specialty chastity device retailers, industrial safety suppliers, and online marketplaces.

### Best practices

- **Take clear photos**: Ensure the seal number is clearly readable in every verification picture
- **Keep the seal intact**: Never attempt to remove or tamper with the seal between verifications
- **Stock up**: Purchase multiple seals in advance, especially if you plan to use hygiene openings
- **Document the number**: Note down your current seal number in case you need to reference it
- **Replace after openings**: Always use a new seal after each hygiene opening or lock removal
