:::tip

:::

## Introduction

AI Keyholder lets you chat with an AI acting as your keyholder. It responds to your messages in character and can act on your lock: add or remove time, change timer visibility, put you in the pillory, or freeze/unfreeze your lock.

## Getting access

The extension is currently reserved to **Chaster Plus** members and limited to testers during the alpha.

1. Go to the [extension page](https://chaster.app/extensions/ai-keyholder-alpha).
2. Click **Join as tester**. You'll see a warning that the extension is in a testing phase and may contain bugs or unexpected behavior.
3. Once you've joined, add it to a lock like any other extension.

## What it can do

Right now, the AI keyholder can:

- Add or remove time
- Change timer visibility
- Put you in the [pillory](/features/pillory)
- Freeze or unfreeze your lock

More actions are planned as the extension develops.

## Choosing your AI model

You can pick which LLM powers your keyholder. Each has a different cost and a different feel:

- **DeepSeek Chat** ($): cheapest option, good for quick, casual exchanges. Tends to lose depth over longer conversations.
- **Grok 4.3** ($$): longer context and stronger reasoning, holds up better in longer or more complex conversations.
- **Claude Sonnet 4.6** ($$$): most expensive, but gives the most vivid writing and the most distinct, in-character keyholder personality.

## Personas

Your keyholder's personality is defined by a persona. You can pick from a list of predefined personas, or create your own with a custom name, caption, description, avatar, and personality traits. Predefined personas can't be edited, but your own personas can be edited or deleted at any time.

## Personalizing your keyholder

Two ways to help the AI understand you:

- **About you**: a free-text field where you describe yourself and anything the AI should keep in mind during conversations.
- **Memory**: the AI automatically remembers relevant details from your conversations over time, things like your preferences, your BDSM/chastity dynamic, your kinks and limits, and your toys and equipment. You can review what it remembers and delete individual memories from the Memories tab. Memories can't be edited directly, only deleted.

## Tasks

The AI can assign you tasks. If you have the [Tasks extension](/extensions/tasks) installed, tasks are created there; otherwise, they're tracked directly within AI Keyholder.

## Quota & pricing

Usage is tracked with a quota, shown in the extension so you can follow how much you're using. It's **free during the alpha**. Once the extension leaves alpha, it will become a paid extension, with a free monthly quota for Chaster Plus members.
