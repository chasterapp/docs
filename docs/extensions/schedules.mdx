With schedules, choose exactly when an extension can be used: a shower window every morning, a spin of the wheel on weekend evenings, or a single extra opening on a special day.

:::caution Feature preview
Schedules are currently in **preview** and are being rolled out progressively. You may not see them in the app yet.
:::

## Introduction

Most extensions let you act again after a waiting time, or earn actions over time (see [Modes](/extensions#modes)). With **On a schedule**, actions are only available inside the windows you choose instead. Each window gives a number of actions, for example one opening between 07:00 and 07:45.

These extensions can run on a schedule:

| Extension | One action is |
| --- | --- |
| [Dice](/extensions/dice) | a roll |
| [Wheel of Fortune](/extensions/wheel-of-fortune) | a spin |
| [Tasks](/extensions/tasks) | a task |
| [Verification picture](/extensions/verification-picture) | a picture |
| [Hygiene opening](/extensions/hygiene-opening) | an opening |

Community extensions can also offer it. When they do, **On a schedule** appears in their settings like for the extensions above.

An action is counted when it starts. For example, an opening started at 07:40 in a window that ends at 07:45 still lasts the full time allowed by the Hygiene opening settings: the schedule decides when you can start, not when you must finish.

:::info
Actions don't carry over. An action you don't use before its window ends is lost, and the next window starts again with its own count.
:::

## Configuration

When you add an extension that supports schedules, its settings first ask how often the action can happen. Choose **On a schedule**. You can also change it later in the **How often** block of the extension settings.

A new schedule starts with one window: every day, all day, with one action. Edit it or add more with **Add a time**. The schedule only takes effect once you save the extension settings.

<img src="/img/extension-schedules-config.png" alt={`Hygiene opening settings with "On a schedule" selected, the "Times follow" choice, and two times: Morning shower every day from 07:00 to 07:45, and After sports every Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday from 18:00 to 21:00`} className="img-phone" />

### Times

Each time in the list is one entry of the schedule:

- **Name (optional)**: a short label shown everywhere the window appears, such as "Morning shower" or "After sports".
- **Repeats**: **Once** on a given date, **Every day**, or **On some days** of the week. With **On some days**, pick the days and choose to repeat every week, every two weeks, and so on. **Add a start or end date** limits a repeating time to a period.
- **Time**: **All day**, or a **Time range** with a start and an end. If the end is earlier than the start, the window ends the next day, for example from 22:00 to 02:00.
- **How many**: the number of actions available in each window, from 1 to 99.

<img src="/img/extension-schedules-time-form.png" alt={`New time form with the name "After sports", repeating on some days (Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday) every week, from 18:00 to 21:00, with 1 opening`} className="img-phone" />

When two windows overlap, an action uses the window that closes first.

### Times follow

On a shared lock, choose whose clock the times follow:

- **The wearer's clock**: 07:00 means 07:00 wherever the wearer lives. This is the default.
- **My clock**: the times follow your own clock and are converted to the wearer's time when they join.

On a lock you set up for yourself or for a specific wearer, times follow the wearer's clock. The time zone is set when the schedule starts: it doesn't change if someone travels or updates their profile later.

### Before joining

On a shared lock page, wearers see each time of the schedule and a preview of the next 7 days, in their own time.

<img src="/img/extension-schedules-public-lock.png" alt={`Hygiene opening on a shared lock page, with "Times follow your clock", the Morning shower and After sports times, and the next 7 days`} className="img-phone" />

## During the lock

Tap the info button at the top of the extension page to see whether a window is open, how many actions are left, and when to use them. When nothing is open, it shows when the next window starts.

<img src="/img/extension-schedules-drawer.png" alt={`Hygiene opening information panel showing "1 opening left, start before 23:59" and the View schedule link`} className="img-phone" />

**View schedule** opens the schedule page:

- At the top, what is open now and what is left, or when the next window starts.
- A line telling you who can change the schedule, for example "You can ask Aaron for changes."
- **Agenda**: the upcoming windows, day by day. **Go to date** jumps to any day.
- **Entries**: the times of the schedule, grouped into repeating and one-time times.
- **History**: past windows, including the ones that were not used.

<img src="/img/extension-schedules-page.png" alt={`Schedule page of a Hygiene opening extension, with 1 opening left until 23:59, the line "You can ask Aaron for changes", and the agenda showing an Evening break window open now and the Morning shower window the next day`} className="img-phone" />

When the wearer and the keyholder live in different time zones, times are shown in the clock the schedule follows, with your own time underneath.

## Changing the schedule

### Who can edit it

The **Edit schedules** [permission](/locks/permissions) decides who can change a running lock's schedule directly. Nobody has it under the Standard preset. The **Trusted keyholder** preset gives it to the keyholder, and **Full trust** gives it to both.

Turning an extension into a schedule on a running lock needs both **Manage extensions** and **Edit schedules**. Turning it back to another mode only needs **Manage extensions**. Once the lock is running, the times are changed from the schedule page, not from the extension settings.

### Editing windows

If you hold **Edit schedules**, tap a window in the agenda to **Edit** or **Remove** it, or tap **+** next to a day to add a one-time window. When you change a repeating window, choose what the change applies to:

- **This occurrence**: only that day.
- **This and following**: that day and every later one.
- **All occurrences**: all upcoming windows. Past windows stay in History.

<img src="/img/extension-schedules-apply-to.png" alt={`"Save recurring window" dialog with the choices This occurrence, This and following, and All occurrences`} className="img-phone" />

Changing a window never resets the actions already used in it. The other person receives a notification describing the change.

### Requesting a change

Without **Edit schedules**, you can still ask for changes on a lock with a keyholder. The same buttons read **Request a window**, **Propose a change** or **Ask to remove**, and the form ends with **Send request**. Nothing changes until the other person approves.

Your request appears in the agenda as **Proposed**, and the other person receives a notification. They open the request to see the change next to the rest of that day, then:

- **Approve** applies the change.
- **Decline** leaves the schedule unchanged.
- **Suggest another time** declines the request and sends a different time back for you to review.

<img src="/img/extension-schedules-review.png" alt={`Review of a request from Valentin to add a "Long run" window on Sunday 15 November from 10:00 to 12:00, shown as Proposed next to the Morning shower window, with the Suggest another time, Decline and Approve buttons`} className="img-phone" />

Until it is answered, you can **Edit request** or **Withdraw** it. You can send several requests at once, and each is answered separately.

:::info
A request expires when the window it is about has passed. Pending requests also close when the keyholder changes or the lock ends. In both cases, nothing changes on the schedule.
:::

On a lock without a keyholder, there is nobody to ask: the schedule can only change if you gave yourself **Edit schedules** when creating the lock.
