---
title: Wheel of Fortune
sidebar_label: Wheel of Fortune
---

Wheel of Fortune lets the wearer spin a wheel during a lock. Each segment has an outcome chosen when the extension is configured. A spin can add or remove time, change whether the lock is frozen, put the wearer in the pillory, or show a custom instruction.

Choose at least two segments and set the duration for outcomes that add or remove time or use the pillory. The wheel starts with one segment that adds an hour and one that removes an hour. The lock's action schedule controls how often the wearer can spin, or spins can be unlimited.

If you include a pillory segment, enable the [pillory](/features/pillory) on the lock so that outcome can take effect.
